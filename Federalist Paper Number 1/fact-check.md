### Paraphrases
Faithful to the paper: the "societies of men… reflection and choice vs. accident and
force" framing; the two classes of opponents (state officeholders + men of perverted
ambition); the candor/concession passage; the "torrent of passions" prediction and the
"despotism/energy of government" symmetry; the demagogue warning; the honest self-
disclosure; and the six-topic outline (script lines 145–155), which is a plain-English
rendering of the source's capitalized list of the six "interesting particulars" (union's
utility, insufficiency of the present Confederation, necessity of an equally energetic
government, conformity to true republican principles, analogy to state constitutions,
additional security for liberty/property). No paraphrase overstates the source.
