## Episode Description
Written by Alexander Hamilton, Federalist No. 1 argues that the Constitution debate is a test of whether free people can establish good government through reason and choice — or whether they'll be ruled by accident and force. In this episode, we break down what the essay was trying to persuade New Yorkers of, why Hamilton thought the stakes were historic, and what his warnings about demagogues still mean today.

## Alternate Short Description
Hamilton opens the Federalist Papers by naming the real question behind the Constitution debate: can a free people govern themselves through reflection and reason, or do they always end up at the mercy of fear, manipulation, and force?
