# Federalist No. 40 Explained: Who Said You Could Do This

## Episode Description
Fifty five men were sent to Philadelphia to fix one document, and they came home with a different document entirely, written behind closed doors. This episode is about the charge that follows from that, which is not that the Constitution is bad but that it is stolen. It opens with the two New York delegates who walked out of the convention over exactly this question and published their reasons in the New York papers four days before Madison answered them, then goes to the convention floor for the afternoon in June when the delegates argued about their own authority out loud, and then follows Madison's three separate answers, which get shorter and better as they go.

## Short Episode Description
The convention was told to revise the Articles of Confederation and it wrote a new Constitution instead, and this is Madison explaining why that was allowed.

## Alternate Short Description
Two of New York's three delegates walked out because they thought the convention had no right to do what it was doing, and this is the paper written to answer them.
