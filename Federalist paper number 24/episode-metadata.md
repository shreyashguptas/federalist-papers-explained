# Federalist No. 24 Explained: The Army That Scared Everyone

## Episode Description
A standing army in peacetime was the single thing Americans of the founding generation feared most, and the new Constitution did not ban it. In this episode, Hamilton meets that fear head on. First he shows that the outcry is almost dishonest, because the new plan is actually more careful about armies than the state constitutions or the old government ever were, adding a rule that the army must be re-funded by elected representatives every two years. Then he argues that a flat ban would have been foolish anyway, because a young nation boxed in by Britain and Spain genuinely needed frontier troops and a navy. The answer the founders landed on was never no army, but an army the voters can starve at will.

## Short Episode Description
The Constitution let the new government keep an army in peacetime, the very thing Americans feared as tyranny, and this is Hamilton explaining why that was the safe choice, not the dangerous one.

## Alternate Short Description
Why the founders refused to ban a peacetime army, and instead put it on a two-year leash held by the people's own elected representatives.
