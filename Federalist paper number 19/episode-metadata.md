# Federalist No. 19 Explained: A Thousand Years of Falling Apart

## Episode Description
Last time, the founders performed an autopsy on ancient Greece. This time they walk the same examination into their own century — and find the disease alive and rotting in modern Europe. The thousand-year-old Holy Roman Empire, armed with a glorious list of powers on paper, turns out to be a "nerveless body" the strong plunder and foreign powers quietly edit; Poland is being carved up alive by its neighbors even as these papers are written; and Switzerland survives only by being too small and too scared to fight, until religion finally splits it. Different centuries, identical disease — and the same hard lesson: a union that governs governments instead of people always rots into anarchy among its members, and a longer list of powers on the parchment will never save it.

## Short Episode Description
A thousand-year-old empire that looked mighty on paper and was helpless in fact — plus two more modern unions that fell apart the very same way ancient Greece did.

## Alternate Short Description
Why does a union of proud, independent states keep rotting from the inside? The founders go looking in modern Europe — and find the answer written in German, Polish, and Swiss history.
