# Fact-Check Log — Federalist No. 19 ("The Empire That Couldn't")

Gate 4 web cross-check. Five independent research passes (parallel agents), each running fresh web searches against high-quality sources (Britannica, .edu, Founders Online, Wikipedia, LEO-BW / Baden-Württemberg state history portal, the Historical Dictionary of Switzerland, Oxford fiscal-military history project, BYU scholarly archive). Two independent corroborating sources required for every load-bearing claim.

**Verdict key:** ✅ verified · ⚠️ contested/hedged in script · ❌ wrong (none).

## Part A — Quotes (mechanical, vs. source file)
All 7 set-apart quotes verified word-for-word as exact substrings of `_production/source-texts/federalist-no-19.txt` via `grep -F`:
- "a nerveless body, incapable of regulating its own members…" ✅
- "before the diet can settle the arrangements, the enemy are in the field…" ✅
- "a history of wars between the emperor and the princes and states…" ✅
- "of general imbecility, confusion, and misery" ✅
- "the miniature picture of the deformities of this political monster" ✅
- "Equally unfit for self-government and self-defense…one third of its people and territories" ✅
- "the moment a cause of difference sprang up, capable of trying its strength, it failed" ✅

Source text obtained from the Yale Avalon Project (avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/fed19.asp). Two OCR artifacts in the Avalon scan were corrected to the canonical Federalist spellings in the source file: "Abb[é] de St. Croix" → "Abbe de Saint Croix"; "inbecility/heriditary/accomodating" → "imbecility/hereditary/accommodating".
