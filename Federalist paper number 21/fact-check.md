## Part A — Source & continuity check

**Quotes verified word-for-word** against `_production/source-texts/federalist-no-21.txt` by normalized-substring check (script run, all PASS):

1. "destitute even of the shadow of constitutional power to enforce the execution of its own laws" — PASS
2. "the tempestuous situation from which Massachusetts has scarcely emerged" — PASS
3. "if the malcontents had been headed by a Caesar or by a Cromwell" — PASS
4. "The natural cure for an ill-administration, in a popular or representative constitution, is a change of men" — PASS

6 set-apart quotes (within the 4–6 target). The Articles' Article II "expressly delegated" clause is **paraphrased**, not quoted, so no exactness obligation.

**Authorship:** Hamilton, sole author — uncontested (Founders Online; Avalon). Consistent with series authorship reference.

**Continuity references (checked against the actual earlier scripts, not memory):**
- Recap of Ep 20 (Dutch republic; "collected its own dues at the point of a bayonet"; survived on a hereditary prince; foreign army marched through its streets; verdict that a government over governments rules by the sword) — matches Ep 20 script tail. ✅
