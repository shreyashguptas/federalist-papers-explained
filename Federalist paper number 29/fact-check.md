## Part A — Source, continuity, internal consistency

### Exact quotes (mechanical normalized-substring check against source — all PASS)
1. "To render an army unnecessary, ... will be a more certain method of preventing its existence than a thousand prohibitions upon paper." — exact (attribution "he writes," inserted).
2. "to provide for organizing, arming, and disciplining the militia" — exact.
3. "reserving to the states respectively the appointment of the officers, and the authority of training the militia according to the discipline prescribed by Congress" — exact. (Presented as two clearly-fragmented quotes with my connective, NOT as one continuous sentence — the source's intervening "and for governing such part of them..." clause is omitted, so it is not claimed as unbroken.)
4. "mutual intelligence and concert" — exact (embedded).
5. "A tolerable expertness in military movements ... is a business that requires time and practice. It is not a day, or even a week, that will suffice for the attainment of it." — exact.
6. "That army ... can never be formidable to the liberties of the people while there is a large body of citizens, little, if at all, inferior to them in discipline and the use of arms, who stand ready to defend their own rights and those of their fellow-citizens." — exact.
7. "Where in the name of common-sense, ... are our fears to end if we may not trust our sons, our brothers, our neighbors, our fellow-citizens?" — exact.
8. "Gorgons, hydras, and chimeras dire" — exact.
9. "This mutual succor ... is, indeed, a principal end of our political association." — exact.

Substantial set-apart quotes: 7 (under the ceiling of 8). None back-to-back.

Consistent (23–29 is the standard common-defense cluster; 29 is the last before taxation begins in 30).
- Removed an earlier "for six papers" count that could conflict with the "Twenty-Three through Twenty-Nine" (seven) framing — now phrased "across this whole run of papers." Swept: 0 remaining "six/seven papers".
- Lexington/Concord dated "seventeen seventy-five"; distance from "seventeen eighty-eight" corrected from "barely a dozen years" to "a little more than a dozen years" (~13 yrs). Swept.
- Publication line hedged (see Part B claim 1); single occurrence, swept.
