## Episode Description
Written by Alexander Hamilton, Federalist No. 6 argues that separate American states would not remain at peace with each other for long, because ambition, commercial rivalry, and the private passions of leaders drive neighboring nations into conflict no matter how republican or how commercial they are. In this episode, we break down Hamilton's case that human nature, historical experience, and recent American events all point toward the same conclusion: division means war.

## Alternate Short Description
Hamilton argues that a divided America would fight itself, because ambition, commerce, and the private passions of leaders make neighboring states natural rivals unless a confederate republic contains them.
