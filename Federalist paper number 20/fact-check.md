# Fact-Check Log — Federalist No. 20 ("The Sister Republic")

Gate 4 web cross-check. Five independent research passes (parallel agents), each running fresh web searches against high-quality sources (Britannica, .edu, Founders Online / National Archives, Library of Congress / Congress.gov, the Yale Avalon primary text, Teaching American History, EH.net, the U.S. State Department Office of the Historian, Erfgoed Leiden, and well-sourced encyclopedia entries). Two independent corroborating sources required for every load-bearing claim.

**Verdict key:** ✅ verified · ⚠️ contested/softened in script · ❌ wrong (none).

## Part A — Quotes (mechanical, vs. source file)
All set-apart quotes verified word-for-word as exact normalized substrings of `_production/source-texts/federalist-no-20.txt`:
- "Imbecility in the government; discord among the provinces; foreign influence and indignities; a precarious existence in peace, and peculiar calamities from war." ✅
- "had to be ultimately collected at the point of the bayonet" ✅
- "Under such a government … the Union could never have subsisted, if the provinces had not a spring within themselves, capable of quickening their tardiness, and compelling them to the same way of thinking. This spring is the stadtholder." ✅ (Abbe de Mably, as quoted by Madison)
- "Experience is the oracle of truth; and where its responses are unequivocal, they ought to be conclusive and sacred." ✅
- "a sovereignty over sovereigns, a government over governments, a legislation for communities, as contradistinguished from individuals" ✅
- "substituting violence in place of law, or the destructive coercion of the sword in place of the mild and salutary coercion of the magistracy" ✅
- (indirect, faithful paraphrase, not set apart) "the actual invasion of foreign arms, the crisis of their destiny" ✅

Source text obtained from the Yale Avalon Project (avalon.law.yale.edu/18th_century/fed20.asp). Three obvious scan typos in the Avalon HTML were corrected to the canonical spellings in the source file: subtitle "Insufficiency **fo**" → "of"; "**confedracy**" → "confederacy"; "**distiny**" → "destiny". The all-caps emphasis words in the closing thesis (VIOLENCE/LAW/SWORD/MAGISTRACY) are rendered in normal case in the spoken script for clean TTS delivery; verification is case-insensitive.

## Outcome
No ❌ claims. Seven ⚠️ items (Madison-as-drafter; "richest nation" superlative; the fleet "outnumbered rivals" framing; VOC "first" company; Wisselbank "central" vs "public" bank; religious-tolerance overstatement; stadtholder "male line"; the "four reforms" count) were softened, attributed, or hedged in the script revision and now read honestly. Cleared Gate 4.
