## Episode Description
Where Federalist No. 6 argued in broad philosophical terms that neighboring states naturally fall into conflict, No. 7 names the specific flashpoints. In this episode, we walk through Hamilton's five-part list of exactly what separate American confederacies would fight about: overlapping territorial claims (including the Wyoming Valley and Vermont disputes), commercial rivalry and state tariffs, apportionment of the Revolutionary debt, state laws violating private contracts (the Rhode Island paper-money crisis), and foreign entanglements. Hamilton closes with the Roman maxim divide et impera — divide and rule — warning that any power wishing America harm is already working from that playbook.

## Alternate Short Description
Hamilton gets specific: territory, trade, debt, broken contracts, and foreign alliances. Each is already visible inside the current confederation. Each would escalate to war without the Constitution.
