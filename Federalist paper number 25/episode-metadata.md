# Federalist No. 25 Explained: Who Should Hold the Army

## Episode Description
Almost everyone agreed the young country would need some soldiers. The real fight was over who should hold them: the national government, or the individual states. In this episode, Hamilton argues that the option most people found comforting, keeping the army close to home under the states, is actually the far more dangerous one. It would dump the whole cost of defense onto a few exposed border states, breed armed jealousy between rival neighbors, and put military power in exactly the hands a free people watches least. Then he turns to a deeper point that still lands today: why a constitution should never write down a noble rule it will be forced to break the first time real danger arrives.

## Short Episode Description
Should an army be kept close to home under your own state, or handed to the distant national government, and why is the answer most people find comforting actually the dangerous one?

## Alternate Short Description
Why the founders refused to let the states run their own armies, and why a written ban on a peacetime army would have been a promise the country was doomed to break.
