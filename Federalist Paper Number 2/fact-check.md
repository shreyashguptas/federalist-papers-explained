### Modern parallels / framing
The script's analogies (organizations building systems in a crisis end up with calcified emergency workarounds; the Articles as "a gentleman's agreement between sovereign states") are illustrative and do **not** distort Jay's argument. The script's honest caveats — that Jay's "one united people" omitted enslaved people, Indigenous nations, and non-English immigrant communities, and that Jay paints the Convention more harmoniously than reality — are historically sound and correctly framed as the script's own qualification of the paper, not as Jay's claim.
