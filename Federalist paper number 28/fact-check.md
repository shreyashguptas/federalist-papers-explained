# Fact-check — Federalist No. 28

## Part A — Source and continuity check

**Source of authority:** `_production/source-texts/federalist-no-28.txt` (Yale Avalon transcription, header + full text of Hamilton's No. 28, "For the Independent Journal").

### Exact-quote verification (normalized substring match against the source file)
All fragments below were verified mechanically (lowercased, whitespace-collapsed substring test) as exact substrings of the source. Woven attributions ("he writes," / "he says,") were stripped before matching.

1. "there may happen cases in which the national government may be necessitated to resort to force" — OK
2. "cannot be denied" — OK
3. "maladies as inseparable from the body politic as tumors and eruptions from the natural body" — OK
4. "the simple force of law" — OK
5. "reveries of those political doctors whose sagacity disdains the admonitions of experimental instruction" — OK
6. "the unceasing agitations and frequent revolutions which are the continual scourges of petty republics" — OK
7. "the whole power of the proposed government is to be in the hands of the representatives of the people" — OK (fixed a drift: had "this proposed" → restored "the proposed")
8. "the essential, and, after all, only efficacious security for the rights and privileges of the people, which is attainable in civil society" — OK
9. "that original right of self-defense which is paramount to all positive forms of government" — OK
10. "rush tumultuously to arms, without concert, without system, without resource; except in their courage and despair" — OK
11. "clothed with the forms of legal authority, can too often crush the opposition in embryo" — OK
12. "power being almost always the rival of power, the general government will at all times stand ready to check the usurpations of the state governments, and these will have the same disposition towards the general government" — OK (fixed a drift: had "is almost always" → restored "being almost always")
13. "the people, by throwing themselves into either scale, will infallibly make it preponderate" — OK
14. "the people, without exaggeration, may be said to be entirely the masters of their own fate" — OK
15. "make head with fresh forces" — OK
16. "provided the citizens understand their rights and are disposed to defend them" — OK
17. "may be considered as a disease, for which there can be found no cure in the resources of argument and reasoning" — OK

Paraphrase note: the "select bodies of experienced men" / "cannot be masked under pretenses" watchdog point (source ¶: "Projects of usurpation cannot be masked under pretenses so likely to escape the penetration of select bodies of men, as of the people at large") is rendered as paraphrase, not a set-apart quote, and is faithful to the paper. "Selected bodies of experienced men" also matches the established series phrasing used in Episode 27.

### Internal consistency (script against itself)
- Army-arc counts: opener says Hamilton "has been talking Americans down" for "four papers" (Numbers 24–27); closer says "these five papers, Numbers Twenty-Four through Twenty-Eight." 24–27 = four prior; 24–28 = five total. Consistent.
- Arc breakdown at close: "The first three" (24, 25, 26) + "Number Twenty-Seven" + "Number Twenty-Eight" = 3 + 1 + 1 = 5. Consistent with "five papers."
- "thirteen states" / "thirteen organized governments" — used consistently.
- Two governments / two scales framing — consistent throughout.
- Zero digits and zero em-dashes in the script (mechanical check passed), so no numeric or punctuation artifacts to reconcile.

### Authorship & continuity
- Author: Alexander Hamilton (signed Publius). Correct (see Part B).
- Ep 27 recap (good government earns obedience; sword is the crutch of a failed government) — matches the Episode 27 script.
- "the recurring, public, on-the-record vote that Number Twenty-Six described" — matches Episode 26's thesis.
- Shays' Rebellion "the uprising we walked through back in Numbers Six and Seven" — Shays is covered in Ep 6 (Shays as desperate debtor) and referenced in Ep 7. Verified against those scripts.
- New York/Vermont dispute "the quarrel we met in Number Seven" — Ep 7 covers the New York/Vermont friction. Verified against that script.
- Fed 29 tease (Hamilton, the militia) — correct (see Part B).
