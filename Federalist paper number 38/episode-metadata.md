# Federalist No. 38 Explained: It Only Has to Be Better

## Episode Description
Four days after admitting the Constitution was flawed, Madison picks that admission back up and turns it into a weapon. Fine, he says, it is imperfect. Imperfect compared to what? Nobody is being offered a choice between this plan and perfection; the choice is between this plan and the government America actually has, and every objection being thrown at the new one lands harder on the old one. This episode follows the Athenians who exiled their most honest man for being too obviously honest, the sick patient surrounded by people who will not say what to do instead, and the western empire the existing Congress had already built with no legal authority to build it and nobody saying a word about it.

## Short Episode Description
Madison stops apologizing for the Constitution and starts asking his critics the one question they cannot answer: compared to what?

## Alternate Short Description
A government too weak to do its job does not stay harmless. It either falls apart or breaks its own rules, and Madison shows it had already started.
